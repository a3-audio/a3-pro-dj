## Open research

- **The listening test** ([part C](../knowledge/spatial/motion-perception.md#c-listening-test-one-evening-at-the-system)):
  one evening with 3–5 listeners at three positions. It decides the speed caps, the band defaults and
  how many movers a floor enjoys, and turns the lab's model results into evidence.
- **C0: the band filter in Hz and octaves.** Until the 3D band filter is measured, FREQ and Q cannot be
  given in musical units.
- **The breath on a real floor.** The lab's most robust result (stand still on beat 4, restart on the
  one) has since been built into A³ Motion's FPV flight, which now starts on the lab's defaults
  (breath on, light planets gated to the one). It has not been put through a listening test.
