MJ Overlay Mockups

Flat sketches for FPV phase 2 and the MJ (motion jockey) on the 1280 × 800 Motion screen, in quiet-indigo-2's roles. Sizes are shares of the screen; the dashed circles are a 9 mm fingertip. Sketches, not the built UI — nothing here is in the repo or the skin.

1FPV with guest groups on the floor

Groups are neutral discs whose size is their weight (G → C → H), never a colour: hue on this screen already means a channel, and a guest group is nobody's channel. The dead zone is the only red thing on the floor and the only hatched one, so it reads at a glance as “not there”.

The big flight path is a quiet line with a tick per beat and a longer tick per bar: the DJ can see where in the four-bar lap the ships are without reading a number. Trails fade from the ship, so a bend round a group is visible for about a bar, then gone. The dotted ring is the capture radius.

2Strip says CLIP or ORBIT, and the Page pad

The mode is a shape before it is a word: CLIP is a hollow pill, ORBIT a filled pill with an orbit glyph. Whom a ship escorts is drawn as the same disc the floor shows, so the eye matches strip to floor by size, not by reading “G3”.

The Page pad carries the orbit glyph and stays lit while the channel is in ORBIT, so the hardware says the mode too. Hold + tap is the one two-hand gesture here (pad hand on the channel, the other finger on the place); it is only for choosing whom to escort, never for anything on the beat.

3Place, weigh, move, remove

Four gestures, each one finger: tap the floor, tap a group, drag a group, hold a group. The weight cycle is drawn as growing discs, so where you are in it is seen, not read.

Remove is the only gesture that destroys something, so it is the only slow one: a red ring fills round the finger for about 0.6 s and can be aborted by lifting. Every group's hit area is at least a fingertip (9 mm) even when the disc is drawn smaller.

4MJ auto: the motion jockey is flying

MJ is someone else driving, so it wears the notice blue the PIO clock wears: “not yours, but fine”. What it will do next is shown as dashed ghost paths on the floor and as beat dots filling towards the downbeat it acts on — the DJ sees a move coming a bar ahead, without a sentence.

Taking over is instant and safe (nothing jumps; the ship keeps flying where it is), so it is easy: touch a ship or press its Page pad takes one, TAKE ALL takes four. Handing back is the deliberate direction. Loudness is three fingertip keys drawn as 1/2/3 bars: OFF, HINT (ghosts only, the DJ executes), FLY.

5FREQ and Q as the band that moves

FREQ and Q are drawn as one lit segment on a frequency strip: FREQ slides it, Q stretches it, 3D sets how bright it is. One picture for three knobs, readable as “what moves” without numbers. The small triangle marks the default (3 kHz, 2 octaves).

The research says bands wider than about 1 octave and with energy above 2–4 kHz localise best; the faint zone shows where that is, the red end below 250 Hz is where the crowd hears no path. A band that is too narrow or too low gets an orange edge — the warning colour, not a blocked control.
